# There is no ad slot in the answer

*Search had an auction. Recommendation does not.*

Search had an auction. Recommendation does not.

For twenty-five years, being found meant paying to be found. You bid on a keyword, someone else bid higher, and the results page sorted you both by how much you were willing to spend. Everyone understood the deal. It was expensive and it was legible.

Ask an AI assistant to recommend a plumber, a bakery or an accountant in your town, and something different happens. You get a short list — three names, maybe five — and nothing on it is sponsored. There is no bid. No budget. No rep to call. No slot to buy at any price.

## What decides the list

What a machine can read about you. That is the whole input.

Not your logo. Not how the site looks on a phone. Not the photographs you paid for. What text exists, what it says plainly, and whether a machine is permitted to read it at all.

Most small businesses publish almost nothing that clears that bar. Not because they opted out of anything — because nobody told them there was something to opt into. A site built to impress a person can be almost blank to a machine, and it never occurs to anyone to check, because the site *looks* finished.

## Why this is worth ten minutes of your attention and not more

Two reasons, and I want to be honest about both.

The first is that it will not last. Every recommendation surface in history eventually grew an advertising product. When this one does, the businesses already in the answers will be defending a position and everyone else will be buying one. The gap between those two positions is the entire point.

The second is that the fix is unglamorous and mostly free. It is text on your own website, written plainly, that a machine is allowed to read. There is no trick and no subscription. A capable person can do most of it in an afternoon.

## Check it yourself, and do not take my word for any of this

You do not need me to check it. Open any assistant, ask it to recommend your own trade in your own town, and see whether you appear. That test costs nothing and takes a minute, and it will tell you more than any agency's audit.
